Capture the Flag


Objective: Deactivate enemy lasers and bring the enemy flag
to your capture point. The capture point is located on the
battlements.

Your team receives 10 points for each capture.
The player who captures receives 10 points.

Other Notes: 
Flag carriers drop the flag when they die.
Dropped flags return to the flag room after 60 seconds.
Lasers are deactivated for 45 seconds.

Flag capture rewards full health, armor, and 2/2 grenade restock.
