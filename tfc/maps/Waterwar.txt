Waterwar - CTF

Objective: 
Enter the enemy base to capture their flag.
Take it back to your battlements.
You can drain the enemy water via a switch in
the basement, allowing easier access to the
flag.


Scoring: 
Capture Flag - 10 pts

Other Notes: Offense spawns at the midpoint of
the map, so there should be minimal ovo.
