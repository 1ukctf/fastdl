Voltage_l - Capture The Flag
By Ruger

Objective: Make your way to the enemy base and 
to the flag room. Bring the flag back to your 
base for 10 points.

Other Notes: Flag carriers drop the Flag when
they die. Dropped flags return to their Base
after 60 seconds.

Touching the walls of the coil Kills
everybody regardless of team, the Ramp room 
switch will activate the coil which
kills everything in it for 10 seconds
and takes 20 seconds to recharge.
