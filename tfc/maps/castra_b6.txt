Capture the Flag

Objective:
Enter the enemy base and go to the flagroom.  
Take the flag back to your base's ramp room to capture.
Your team receives 10 points for each capture.
The player who captures receives 10 points.

Other Notes: 
Flag carriers drop the flag when they die.
Dropped flags return to the flag room after 60 seconds.
Grenade bag located in base ramp room.

Flag capture rewards full health, armor, and grenade restock.
