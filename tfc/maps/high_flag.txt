--- High Flag ---

by Highlander- SuperGunn@Hotmail.com

Get into the enemy base and grab the flag out of
their yard. The flag is up high, so you can only get 
it by climbing up the scaffolding or rocket/grenade/
concussion jumping up to it. Once you have the
flag, take it back to your Sniper Deck to capture it.

---Other Notes---

Ten points per capture.
Dropped flags return after 60 seconds.
There is one grenade supply in each base, at the
far side of the flag area.
